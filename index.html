Product Marketing

Position products, understand buyers, and connect product features to market demand.

Best for students who... like strategy, messaging, customer research, sales enablement, and launch planning.

These roles help organizations make better marketing decisions. Early-career work usually includes research, analysis, stakeholder updates, process support, and turning ambiguous requests into usable recommendations.

Core Skills
PositioningMessagingBuyer researchLaunch planningSales enablement
Career Path
PMM Associate -> Product Marketing Manager -> Senior PMM -> Director, PMM
Entry-Level Titles to Try
Product Marketing AssociateAssociate Product Marketing ManagerGTM Specialist
Starter Search Terms
product marketing associateassociate product marketing managerGTM specialistsales enablement specialist
Realistic Entry Points
Realistic entry points include Product Marketing Associate, GTM Specialist, Sales Enablement Specialist, or marketing roles supporting a product team.
How this differs from nearby roles
Different from brand marketing because it is closer to product features, buyer personas, launch messaging, competitive positioning, and sales enablement.
